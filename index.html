devon-uploader

devon-uploader is a private, internal tool. Its owner uses it to upload and schedule videos on the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API.

  • It is used only by the owner of the channels. It has no other users and is not offered to the public.
  • It uploads only original videos made by the owner, with title, description, tags, thumbnail, playlist and publish time.
  • It does not read, collect or share data about other YouTube users.
